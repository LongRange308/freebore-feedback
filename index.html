Thanks for testing Freebore

Freebore is a logbook for precision rifle shooters: rifles and barrels with round counts, a dope book, group measurement from photos, and a range log. It is developed by Michael Leaf.

Send feedback

Bugs, ideas, things that are confusing: all of it helps. For a bug, say what you tapped and what happened.

Please don't include passwords or other private information.

Your data

Freebore keeps everything on your phone. It has no account, no ads and no tracking, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you share a backup, spreadsheet or PDF yourself. This form only sends what you type into it.
